# Phase 11.2: Aangetekende ingebrekestelling - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-18 (--auto mode, all gray areas auto-resolved with recommended defaults)
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A landlord can generate a print-ready formal notice of default (NL "aangetekende ingebrekestelling", FR "mise en demeure", DE "Inverzugsetzung") for a contract in rent arrears, in the tenant's language, and the app keeps proof of it.

The document must carry everything a Belgian justice of the peace expects in an arrears file: sender and addressee postal address, the contract reference, the arrears broken down per month, interest and late fee where the contract provides for them, the payment instruction, a payment deadline, and the warning that judicial dissolution of the lease will be sought if the deadline passes. The landlord posts the letter by registered mail themselves; the app generates, stores and logs it as a `letter` communication so the date of notice is provable later.

In scope: the document renderer, the letter content in NL/FR/DE/EN, the postal address fields needed to address it, the generate endpoint, the download and the "posted on" record in the UI, and replacing `generateLatePaymentPdf()` with the real renderer.

Out of scope: sending registered mail through any carrier API (bpost, eBox, registered e-mail), e-signatures, judicial document templates (dagvaarding, verzoekschrift), and any legal advice feature.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Document rendering
- **D-01:** The deliverable is a real PDF, A4 (210x297 mm), not an HTML print view and not text under a `.pdf` name. The letter ends up in a court file, so it has to be a fixed, paginated document.
- **D-02:** Render with a pure-JS PDF library added to `apps/api` (pdfkit is the default choice; pdf-lib is the fallback if font or layout work gets awkward). Do NOT render through Playwright/Chromium: `playwright` is already a dependency for `smovinScraper.ts`, but launching a browser inside the API process on the shared m1 box to make a letter is the wrong trade, and the Chromium binary is not guaranteed there.
- **D-03:** One shared renderer module (new, e.g. `apps/api/src/services/formalNotice.ts` plus a small `pdfDocument.ts` helper) used by both the formal notice and the final-reminder attachment.
- **D-04:** Fonts must render Dutch, French and German accents correctly. Verify a French and a German letter body before the plan is called done.
- **D-05:** `generateLatePaymentPdf()` in `apps/api/src/services/paymentFollowUp.ts:351` is replaced by the real renderer. Today it returns plain text with `contentType: "application/pdf"` and carries its own TODO: that attachment becomes a genuine PDF in this phase.

### Letter content
- **D-06:** Fixed sections, in this order: sender block, addressee block, place and date, subject line naming it a formal notice of default, the contract reference (property address plus lease start date), the arrears table (period, due date, amount), interest and late fee lines where applicable, the total claimed, the payment instruction (IBAN plus the contract's structured communication), the payment deadline, the consequence paragraph, an optional free paragraph from the landlord, closing and signature block.
- **D-07:** The subject line and one sentence in the body state explicitly that the letter is a formal notice of default, so the document is self-identifying in a court file.
- **D-08:** The consequence paragraph states that if payment is not received by the deadline, dissolution of the lease and eviction will be sought before the justice of the peace, with interest and costs claimed. No threat the landlord cannot legally carry out (no lock changes, no re-letting, no self-help).
- **D-09:** The letter cites no statute article numbers. Belgian obligations law was recodified (Book 5 of the Civil Code, in force since 2023) and a wrong citation in a generated legal document is worse than none. The letter names the contract, the facts, the deadline and the consequence. Legal references stay out of the template.
- **D-10:** Payment deadline: default 15 calendar days from the letter date, overridable per generation in the request. Not a new settings field.
- **D-11:** One optional free-text paragraph the landlord can fill in before generating, inserted after the payment instruction. No full template editor in this phase.

### Address data
- **D-12:** `users` gets a postal address (street, number, box, postal code, city, country default BE) for the sender block, editable in Settings. `tenants` gets an optional correspondence address with the same shape.
- **D-13:** The addressee defaults to the rented property's address from `properties` (street, streetNumber, postalCode, city, country already exist). The tenant's correspondence address overrides it when filled in, which is what makes the letter usable when the tenant has already left or sits elsewhere, including in prison.
- **D-14:** Generation fails with a clear, actionable error when the landlord's postal address is missing, naming the Settings page. Never generate a letter with a blank sender block.
- **D-15:** Schema change goes in with `db:push`, not `db:generate`. The 0000 Drizzle snapshot is stale and `db:generate` replays a non-additive backlog. Production needs the DDL applied in a human-run step, the same pattern as 11-07.

### Trigger, access and API
- **D-16:** `POST /api/v1/payments/formal-notice` with `{ leaseId, deadlineDays?, extraParagraph? }`. Authorisation identical to `POST /api/v1/payments/send-reminder`: property access plus `manager` or higher, write scope for API-token callers, audit-logged.
- **D-17:** The response carries the PDF (or a short-lived download reference) plus the computed totals, so the UI can show what the letter claims before the landlord prints it.
- **D-18:** UI: a button in the arrears/reminder area of the contract detail page, next to the existing reminder actions. It opens a small dialog for deadline and the optional paragraph, then downloads the PDF.

### Logging and proof
- **D-19:** Generating logs a row in `communications` with `channel: "letter"`, `direction: "outbound"`, a new `type` value `formal_notice`, `status: "queued"`, and metadata holding the deadline, the arrears breakdown and the total claimed. `body` holds the rendered letter text so the exact wording is reproducible.
- **D-20:** The landlord can mark the letter as posted: a date and an optional registered-item reference (bpost barcode). That sets `sentAt` and `status: "sent"`. This is the whole legal point of the feature, since the deadline runs from the posting date and the judge asks for that date.
- **D-21:** Adding `formal_notice` to the `communications.type` enum is a schema change, so it rides along with D-15.

### Arrears scope and money
- **D-22:** The letter covers every outstanding month on the contract as of the letter date, not a single month. A formal notice that understates the debt forces a second one.
- **D-23:** Reuse the existing computation, add none: `loadContractBalance()` / `remainderRows` for what is open, `calculateInterest()` for interest, and the lease's `latePaymentFeeEnabled/Amount/Enforcement` for the fee. The amount in the letter must match the amount the final reminder would state.
- **D-24:** Interest and fee appear only when the contract provides for them. No default interest claim on a contract that does not carry one.

### Language
- **D-25:** Letter body templates live in code as defaults per language, following the pattern of the reminder templates in `paymentFollowUp.ts`, selected by `tenants.language`. The landlord's own locale does not pick the letter language.
- **D-26:** Four languages are mandatory (NL, FR, DE, EN). New UI strings go in all four locale files; `apps/api/src/__tests__/i18n-completeness.test.ts` is the gate.

### Research corrections (2026-11-18, after 11.2-RESEARCH.md)

These supersede the earlier decision they name. The researcher executed the code rather than recalling it, so where they conflict, these win.

- **D-27 (supersedes D-02):** pdfkit confirmed, no fallback named. pdfkit 0.20.2 ships a native ESM entry and compiles the standard-14 font metrics into JS, so the tsup bundling failure that worried D-02 cannot occur; verified by building against this project's own tsup config and running the output. pdf-lib's last release is 2022-05-12 and is dropped as the fallback.
- **D-28 (extends D-04):** commit a TTF and register it with `doc.registerFont()`. The built-in Helvetica covers the NL/FR/DE/EN accent set and the euro sign, so the literal D-04 requirement passes, but any name outside Latin-1 (`Lukasz Wojcik` with its real diacritics, `Aysegul` with Turkish letters) corrupts silently with no exception. Liberation Sans Regular plus Bold (SIL OFL 1.1, about 825 KB) with its licence file. Also normalise every string to NFC before drawing: NFD-decomposed input corrupts even with an embedded font.
- **D-29 (extends D-05):** the final-reminder attachment is broken in production today, not merely fake. `generateLatePaymentPdf()` returns a Buffer, `queueEmail` hands it to BullMQ, Redis JSON-serialises it, and the worker gives nodemailer a revived `{type:"Buffer",data:[...]}`, which throws. Reproduced. The fix is to carry attachments as a base64 string with `encoding: "base64"`. D-05 is therefore "make the attachment arrive", not just "make the bytes real".
- **D-30 (supersedes D-15):** do NOT run `drizzle-kit push` on this database. It throws `TypeError: reading 'checkConstraint'` on the project's MariaDB, and `db:generate` replays the non-additive backlog. The proven path is hand-written idempotent DDL applied in a `[BLOCKING]` human-run task, the same pattern as 11-07. The new `communications.type` enum value must be appended LAST: MySQL enum values are ordinal-indexed and there is no `ADD VALUE`.
- **D-31 (supersedes D-17):** the response is JSON carrying the PDF as base64 plus the computed totals. A single binary body cannot carry both, and the API's `cors()` has no `exposeHeaders`, so a header channel is not available either.
- **D-32 (supersedes part of D-23):** the claimed total is `loadContractBalance(...).owed`, and the per-month table comes from `loadContractBalance(...).perMonth`, which already carries `{month, dueDate, expected, received, shortfall, settled, ignored}`. The earlier "the amount must match what the final reminder would state" is arithmetically impossible against D-22: a reminder claims one month capped at the balance, the letter claims the whole debt. `remainderRows.ts` is a sweep job, not an arrears view, and is not the source for the table.
- **D-33 (refines D-24):** interest is an owner-level setting in this codebase, not per lease. Use the owner flag and record the discrepancy rather than inventing a per-lease interest field in this phase.
- **D-34 (refines D-10):** default deadline becomes 16 calendar days from the letter date, still overridable per generation. The letter prints the resulting calendar date. The real dispatch date is captured by D-20, which is what a dispute would turn on.
- **D-35 (settles the co-tenant question D-06 left open):** one individually addressed letter per liable tenant on the contract, each rendered as its own PDF and each logged as its own `communications` row. A formal notice puts the addressee in default, so a notice naming only the primary tenant does not reach a jointly liable co-tenant. The response therefore returns a list, not one document.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The trigger for this phase was a live landlord question: a tenant is in prison for six years, rent stops coming in, and the landlord asked what the software tells them to do. The honest answer was that Rentular sends email, SMS and WhatsApp, which a prison does not answer, and that the first real step in the non-payment track is a registered formal notice the app could not produce. That is the use case to keep in mind: the addressee is often not at the rented property, which is why D-13 exists.
- Legal context behind D-08: for non-payment the landlord cannot terminate out of court. Only the justice of the peace can dissolve the lease, and in practice an arrears case needs roughly two to three months of arrears plus a prior formal notice before a judge will dissolve rather than impose a payment plan. The letter is the document that starts that clock, so its date must be recorded (D-20).
- Document deliverable: the PDF is generated by application code that ships to production, not by a Claude document skill. No catalog skill, no license surface.
</specifics>
