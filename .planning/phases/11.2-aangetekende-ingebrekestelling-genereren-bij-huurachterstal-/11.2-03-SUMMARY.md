## Decisions Made
- **The claim follows the contract balance.** `totalClaimed = rentOwed + interest + fee`, never `tableSum + ...`. When the months do not add up to the balance, the amounts block carries one extra labelled line ("Reeds ontvangen en verrekend" when money came in elsewhere, "Overig openstaand huursaldo" when the balance is higher) so the reader can follow the arithmetic and the letter never claims more than the balance plus interest and fee. This settles research Open Question 1 inside D-32.
- **Interest is injected.** `computeFormalNoticeTotals(input, interestFn)`. The real `calculateInterest` is passed by the orchestrator in plan 11.2-05, so there is still exactly one formula (D-23) while this module stays importable without a database or a queue worker.
- **The free paragraph is sanitised here too.** `buildFormalNotice` runs `sanitiseExtraParagraph` itself, so the 20-line cap and the control-character strip hold even if a future caller forgets.
- **The envelope names a country only when the parties are in different ones**, which is how a Belgian letter is addressed; when they differ, both blocks get a country line.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The plan's D-09 citation regex false-positives on the Dutch month name**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (the citation test, on the twelve-month Dutch letter)
- **Issue:** The plan specifies `/art(icle|ikel)?\.?\s*\d/i` over the letter body. "maart 2026" (March, from `formatMonthYear`) contains "art" directly before a number, so the test failed on correct, citation-free copy. The regex as written cannot pass for any Dutch letter covering March.
- **Fix:** Added a word boundary (`/\bart(icle|ikel)?\.?\s*\d/i`) and, to keep D-09 coverage from weakening, a second assertion rejecting "art", "artikel", "article" and their plurals as standalone words. A real citation ("art. 1728bis", "artikel 224", "Article 5") still fails the test.
- **Cost if wrong:** If the boundary were too loose, a genuine statute citation could slip into a court document unnoticed. The added standalone-word check covers that: a citation needs the word, and the word now fails on its own.
- **Files modified:** apps/api/src/services/__tests__/formalNoticeText.test.ts
- **Verification:** Ran the file: 61 tests pass, including the citation test over four languages and four branch combinations (including the twelve-month table that exposed the problem).
- **Commit:** `15eef9a`

## Assumption Drift (advisory)

**1. The plain-text body includes the table header row**
- **Found during:** Task 2

- **Actual:** The body prints the header line ("Periode, Vervaldag, Openstaand bedrag") above those lines.

- **Cost if wrong:** Cosmetic. One extra line in `communications.body`; no amount, date or legal sentence changes.
